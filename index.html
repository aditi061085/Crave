Privacy Policy
Last updated March 2026

1. What We Collect

We collect your email address or Google account info when you sign in. We also temporarily process menu photos you upload to generate recommendations.

2. How We Use It

Your information is used only to provide the Crave service. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers.

We request your location only to find nearby restaurants. Location data is not stored after your session ends.
